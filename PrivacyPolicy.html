Privacy Policy

CivicNow is designed to make civic information easier to access while respecting your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Our Privacy Approach

CivicNow is a civic engagement platform created by UMass Boston students. Our goal is to help Massachusetts residents quickly identify their elected representatives and learn how to engage with them.

We designed the platform to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information We Collect

  • No account creation is required to use CivicNow.
  • We do not store personal information from users browsing the site.
  • Addresses entered on the site are not saved by CivicNow.
  • The platform simply helps locate publicly available government information.

Surveys and Feedback

We may occasionally collect feedback through surveys to understand civic awareness and improve the platform.

  • Surveys may be hosted on external platforms such as Google Forms.
  • Those services may have their own privacy policies.
  • Survey responses are used only for research or improving CivicNow.

External Government Links

CivicNow may link to official government websites so users can access accurate information about elections and representatives.

Examples include state and federal government websites that provide verified public information.

These websites operate independently and maintain their own privacy policies.

Policy Updates

Because CivicNow is an evolving academic project, this privacy policy may be updated if new features are added in the future.
