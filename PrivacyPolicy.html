Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CivicNow collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website.

Introduction

CivicNow is a civic engagement platform designed to help Massachusetts residents find their elected representatives and access voter information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing the amount of data collected from visitors.

Information We Collect

CivicNow is designed so users can access information without creating an account or submitting personal details.

  • No user registration is required.
  • We do not collect names or phone numbers.
  • We do not maintain personal user profiles.

Address Searches

When you enter an address to locate your representatives, the address is used only to determine the appropriate government districts and representatives associated with that location.

Addresses entered into the search tool are not permanently stored.

Email Communication

If you contact CivicNow through email, we may receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not shared or sold.

Third-Party Links

CivicNow may provide links to external websites such as government agencies or official election resources.

These websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies.

Website Analytics

CivicNow may use basic analytics tools to understand general website traffic patterns.

These tools may collect anonymous information such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate geographic region.

This data does not personally identify visitors.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect our website and systems from unauthorized access or misuse.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

CivicNow may update this Privacy Policy as the platform evolves.

When updates occur, the revised version will be posted on this page.
