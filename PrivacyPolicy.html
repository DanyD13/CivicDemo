Privacy Policy

CivicNow is designed to make civic information easier to access while respecting your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

CIVICNOW POLICIES

Your Privacy Matters

CivicNow was created by UMass Boston students to help Massachusetts residents easily find their elected officials and learn how to engage with government. We designed the platform to collect as little personal information as possible.

🔒

Our Privacy Approach

  • No accounts or sign-ups required
  • Designed to minimize personal data collection
  • Focused only on civic information access
📍

Information We Collect

  • Addresses entered to locate representatives
  • Data is used only to retrieve public district information
  • We do not permanently store entered addresses
📝

Surveys & Feedback

  • Occasional feedback surveys may be shared
  • Some surveys may use external platforms
  • Responses help improve the CivicNow platform
🏛

External Government Links

  • CivicNow links to official government websites
  • These sites operate independently
  • They maintain their own privacy policies
🔄

Policy Updates

  • This privacy policy may evolve as CivicNow develops
  • Updates will reflect new features if added
  • Last updated: 2026
