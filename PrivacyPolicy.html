Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information CivicNow collects and how it is used.

Overview

CivicNow is a student-built civic engagement platform created by UMass Boston students. Our goal is to help Massachusetts residents quickly identify their elected representatives and learn how to engage with them.

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible while providing useful civic resources.

Information We Collect

CivicNow does not require users to create accounts or log in. The website itself does not store personal information from visitors browsing the platform.

If users choose to participate in surveys or external forms linked on the site, those responses may be collected by the third-party platform hosting the survey.

External Links

CivicNow may link to official government websites or other informational resources to provide users with accurate election and representative information.

These websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies when visiting external sites.

How Information Is Used

Any survey responses or feedback collected through the project are used only for academic research and to help improve the CivicNow platform.

We do not sell, share, or distribute personal information to third parties.

Changes to This Policy

Because CivicNow is an academic project that may continue evolving, this privacy policy may be updated if new features are added in the future.
